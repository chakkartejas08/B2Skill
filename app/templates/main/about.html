About

An AI-powered bridge between small business and emerging talent.

B2Skill AI isn't just another freelancing marketplace. Most small businesses know they need to "go digital" but don't know what that actually means — a website, a booking system, better social media, or something else entirely. At the same time, students learning real skills often struggle to find real projects to practice on.

We built B2Skill AI to solve the problem before the marketplace even starts: analyze the business's goal, turn the gaps into a prioritized plan, and connect it with a student who's actually equipped to help.
